# Miami

Part of [[_state]] · County: [[miami-dade-county]] · Hub: this is a hub city

## Demographics

- Population: 489,812 (July 1, 2025 estimate); 442,241 at the 2020 census; 399,457 in 2010. Up 10.8% from the 2020 estimates base to 2025, against 3.1% for the U.S. [1]
- Median household income: $62,462 (2020–2024, in 2024 dollars). Florida $74,568, Miami-Dade County $71,753, U.S. $80,734. [1]
- Per capita income $45,837; 19.4% of people in poverty (U.S. 10.6%). [1]
- Census place code (GEOID) 1245000, from QuickFacts. [1]

## Industries and major employers

- PortMiami: 8,564,225 cruise passengers in fiscal year 2025 (Oct 2024–Sep 2025), up 4.02% and a record; 1,115,058 cargo TEUs, up 2.35%, 11th straight year above one million. [2]
- PortMiami states it contributes about $61 billion a year to the local economy and supports more than 340,000 jobs in South Florida. Page does not give the year of the estimate. [3]
- County-wide (not city-only) employer list for 2024, unaudited: Miami-Dade County Public Schools 35,497; Miami-Dade County government 29,495; University of Miami 22,566; Baptist Hospital of Miami 5,469; Baptist Health South Florida 4,919. [4]
- County-wide: education and health services was the second-largest industry, 215,400 jobs in December 2024. [5]
- Sectors named in the sources: cruise and port trade, healthcare, education. Tourism, logistics and construction as employers: None found from a primary source (searched 2026-11-05).
