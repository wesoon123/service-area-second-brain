## Events and incidents

- December 30, 2013: a westbound BNSF grain train derailed and was hit by an eastbound BNSF crude oil train of about 106 cars carrying about 700 barrels each; the crude burned. Evacuation figures differ by source (about 1,400 people per the NTSB-based report; up to about 2,300–2,400 told to flee); no one was hurt. [1][2][3]
- February 2017: the NTSB found a broken axle caused the derailment and criticized crew protection on the oil train. [4]
