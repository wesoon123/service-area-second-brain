## Events and incidents

- June 2, 2024: flash flood in Stutsman County near Jamestown, property damage estimated at $0.5 million (NWS storm data); an 88 mph gust was reported a few miles east-southeast of Jamestown. [1]
- June 20–21, 2025: statewide severe storms; Otter Tail power outage map showed about 8,300 Jamestown-area properties without power that afternoon. FEMA later awarded more than $846,000 in public assistance to North Dakota communities for these storms. [2][3]
- August 7–8, 2025: NWS damage survey of thunderstorm wind damage in Jamestown; police reported wind took the roof off the Spitzer Construction building. [4]
