## Events and incidents

- Feb 29, 2024: Missouri River ice jam caused minor flooding in Bismarck and Mandan, the first ice jam at minor flood stage in Bismarck since 2009. The governor declared an emergency for Burleigh and Morton counties; the National Guard flew Black Hawk helicopters with 660-gallon buckets to break the jam and Garrison Dam releases were cut. [1][2]
- June–July 2024: summer storms; hail fell in north Bismarck on July 29, 2024, and the governor declared a summer storm disaster in central North Dakota. [3]
