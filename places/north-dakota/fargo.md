## Events and incidents

- June 20–21, 2025: severe storms across North Dakota; in Fargo, flash flooding stalled cars in underpasses and overwhelmed storm sewers. The governor declared a statewide disaster on June 21, 2025 (executive order 2025-06); four storm-related deaths statewide and nearly 37,000 people lost power. [1][2]
- April 2023: spring flooding; statewide emergency declared April 10, 2023, and the Red River crested at Fargo at 29.74 feet. [4][5]
- March 2009: Red River flood, blamed for two deaths statewide; a winter storm followed during recovery. [3] (outside the 5-year window, context)
