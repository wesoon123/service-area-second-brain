## Climate risks

- Blizzards, spring and summer flooding, tornadoes, straight-line wind, hail, ice-jam flooding and wildfire. Governor emergency or disaster orders: April 2022 (blizzard, storms, flooding), April 10, 2023 (spring flooding), February 29, 2024 (Missouri River ice jam, Burleigh and Morton counties), summer 2024 (central North Dakota storms), June 21, 2025 (statewide disaster, executive order 2025-06).
- June 20–21, 2025 storms: statewide, four deaths, nearly 37,000 without power, a tornado near Enderlin rated EF-3 at first and later upgraded to EF-5 per one report (verify), state request to FEMA cited public damage of nearly $11.5 million. [1]
